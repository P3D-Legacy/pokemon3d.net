## Game

Pokémon 3D is developed by the Pokémon 3D Team and the wider community. For questions about the game, join [our Discord server](https://pokemon3d.net/redirect/discord) and chat with the team and other players.

## Support

We do not offer personal one-to-one support for the game. Please ask in [our Discord server](https://pokemon3d.net/redirect/discord), where the community is usually quick to help. If nobody there can answer, the team will follow up when they can.

## Website

This website is open source and maintained by the Pokémon 3D Team and community contributors. You are welcome to suggest changes or contribute on [GitHub](https://github.com/P3D-Legacy/pokemon3d.net).

For website feedback or issues, Discord is still the best place to reach us.
